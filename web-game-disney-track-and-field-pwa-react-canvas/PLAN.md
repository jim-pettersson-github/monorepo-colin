# Sagospelen

## Implemented

Twenty individual athletics events with four competitors, one or two human players, three AI difficulties, hold-to-run and alternating-button styles. Eight locally drawn Disney characters. Painted stadium artwork, animated characters, camera tracking, hurdles/water obstacles, jump bars, field implements, countdowns and results.

Quick events and a six-event individual championship. Three-attempt field events, separate hold/rhythm personal bests, validated saves with backup, keyboard/touch/gamepad controls with mapping, pause on background/disconnect, fullscreen, procedural sound and offline PWA updates.

## Verification

Simulation tests complete every event, check timing/input, fouls, tresteg, hurdles, championship points, pause, records and corrupted saves. Browser checks cover the catalogue, character choices, keyboard/touch, resume, portrait/landscape, saved results, subpath manifest and offline play.

## Remaining review

Physical Android installation, USB/Bluetooth controllers, sound comfort and playtesting/tuning. Wi-Fi multiplayer, official judging, full-length endurance races, relays/team sports and additional characters are not implemented.
