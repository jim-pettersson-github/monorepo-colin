# Plan

## Done

- Wi-Fi career sessions with per-player money, cars and upgrades, saved on the host and resumable; late joiners start fresh.
- Short pairing codes with share/copy/paste fallback.
- WebRTC races for 2–8 devices with two-way QR pairing, guest car choice, shared pause and separate local cameras; career remains local.

- Six tracks: two circuits, two city tracks and two country roads.
- Career mode with cups, prize money, car classes, upgrades and rivals who upgrade too.
- Nineteen cars from the requested Need for Speed III PC racer list, with distinct procedural silhouettes and rating-derived arcade performance.
- Tyre smoke, grass dust, barrier sparks and lens flare.
- Published on GitHub Pages under /charlie-fs/.
- Single race against seven AI drivers, laps, positions, lap records and results.
- Arcade driving model, chase/bumper cameras, 90s-style HUD with live standings and name tags above the cars.
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls; synthesised sound.

## Ideas for later

- Reverse and mirrored track variants.
- Time trial with a ghost car.
- Night or rain versions of the tracks.
- Car colour choice and selling cars.
- Physical phone and gamepad checks.
